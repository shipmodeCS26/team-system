## 1. The heart of the brand (read this before writing any page)

### 1.1 The brand core (locked by Carlos, October 27, 2026)

- **We're not selling escape. We're selling presence.**
- **Their lives are already epic.** The mom on the school run, the BJJ guy, the person at the desk. They don't want to change their lives. They want to be more present in them.
- **The customer is the hero. Rhēn is the spark.** The epic comes from the people, never from the brand.
- **Language lock.** "Spark" is the brand verb, "fuel" is the copy word, and "bring you back to it" also works. *Your Rhēn is your moment. Your mood is how you meet it.*

**Manifesto:** *Your life doesn't need fixing. The school run. The mat. The late night at the desk. It's already good. But you're tired. You're stuck in your head. You're running on empty. Rhēn is the spark that brings you back. Not an escape from your life. Fuel for living it. What's your Rhēn?*

**Promise:** *Present today. Stronger tomorrow.* · **Tagline:** *Not more energy. Better energy.*

**Personality:** elegant, but we dare. Fancy, human, witty. **Voice:** dry, kind, brief. Examples: "Tear. Stir. Proceed." · "Be here now. Tomorrow can text you." · "One pouch. That's the whole range."

### 1.2 How we write: scene first, then fact

Generic supplement copy lists features ("contains X, Y, Z for sustained energy"). Copy built on avatar research opens with the customer's **real scene**, uses **their own words**, names **what they've already tried**, and then gives a **reason to believe**. Every page on the site follows this order. Rhēn adapts the method to its own rules:

| Keep from the method | Drop: these break Rhēn's rules |
|---|---|
| A specific scene and time ("6:45", "2 PM", "3 AM") | Guilt or shame (parenting guilt, "resent bedtime stories"). The ICP research rules out guilt and invented hidden fears. |
| Customer language taken from the avatar research quotes | Disease or body-mechanism claims ("it's your hypothalamus", "inflammation"). These go beyond structure/function claims. |
| "You've tried X, Y, Z" (coffee, cans, separate pills and tubs) | "Your doctor is wrong" framing |
| A reason to believe: exact doses, the label, the COA | Pregnancy, postpartum or breastfeeding angles (a hard rule for the Maya avatar) |
| The customer stays the hero | "We fix your broken life". Their life is already good, and Rhēn brings them back to it. |

### 1.5 Words and claims

- **Use:** better, clear, steady, mellow, focused, sharp, present. "Supports focus." "Mellow, focused energy." "5 g creatine to support strength and performance."\* "Real ingredients, real doses."
- **Never:** calm, help, fix, ritual [OPEN, see 4.2], sleep, anxiety or stress relief, "no jitters, no crash", withdrawal or quitting, ADHD, menopause, pregnancy, weight loss, disease, "clinically studied", "clinically proven", mortality or heart wording.
- Research education (two clocks, label literacy, keep the morning cup) goes on a **Learn** page only, with citations and after Team Lead and Quill review.
- \*Any line that needs the FDA disclaimer gets the asterisk. The disclaimer also sits in the site footer.

### 1.6 Who we're talking to at launch

- **Avatars doc, top 3:** Maya (lead), the quitter (conversion engine), Renée (credibility and word of mouth). Runner-up: the desk builder.
- **ICP research:** the primary test is the convenience-led current creatine buyer. Compare against interested new creatine buyers. Treat parents, desk workers and active life as creative *contexts*, not as separate audiences.
- **How the site combines them:** the hero and story bands use the avatar scenes. The product page leads with one buying reason, "a complete daily blend in a portable stick, with explicit amounts", and then shows every fact.

### 1.7 Look (from the Brand Package, which is the source of truth for visuals)

- Colors: Mineral White `#F5F5F3` (background), Pure White `#FFFFFF`, Soft Neutral `#DEDEDB`, Stone Gray `#8C8C88`, Charcoal `#1F1F1E`, and **Chrome Lime `#C8F23D`** with 2 to 3 moments per screen (one primary button, one key word, one status dot). No beige, cream or blue tints.
- Type: DM Sans only. The wordmark is the only serif and is never retyped.
- Photos: grainy, film-camera shots of real people in daily moments, with small future details. The bar is the kitchen-counter reference photo.
- Honest commerce: no fake "was" prices, "most loved" badges or countdown timers. Subscriptions have clear terms, skip, pause or cancel in a couple of clicks, and a reminder before every renewal.

## 4. Conflicts the owners need to settle

1. **Creatine dose:** the operating plan and the deck say 3 g. The avatars doc, ICP research and Nick's docs say 5 g, and the ICP research says 5 g replaces the older 3 g. The final label decides.
2. **The word "ritual":** it is banned in brand copy (Carlos, Oct 27), but the Brand Package keeps "Build your rituals" and the deck uses it.
3. **"30-Day Guarantee":** it appears on the checkout logo, while the refund policy draft says no returns on opened product. Nick's docs hold guarantee hooks until terms exist.
4. **$79.95:** this price was seen on the live Presence page, but the plan sets no price until the unit economics in section D are done.
5. **Visual direction:** the ICP research mentions cream, serif headings and sunrise orange. The Brand Package says no cream, DM Sans only, and Chrome Lime. This plan follows the Brand Package unless Carlos says otherwise.
6. **"No cans" in photos:** does this mean drink cans and not THE CAN?
7. **"Free shipping on every order":** shipping cost per order is still TBD.
