SHIPMENT DETAILS
LIVE TRACKING SETUP

Connect your shipment data

The workspace is ready to review. Automatic monitoring needs these connections before it can protect real shipments.

  1. Private access & permanent storage

    Connect a PostgreSQL database and configure your workspace sign-in. Real imports remain disabled until then.

  2. Import existing shipments

    Start with a ShipSidekick export mapped to the shipment template. Include every open shipment, even when its order is fulfilled.

  3. Connect carrier tracking updates

    Configure verified ShipSidekick tracking webhooks for each client. Test with a real label-created shipment and a shipment that has moved.

Webhooks alone do not backfill old shipments. A reconciliation feed and reliable hosting are needed to catch missed updates. No email or background alerts are active yet.
